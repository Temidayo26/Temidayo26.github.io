AI training & evaluation

Making AI models more accurate and safer to use.

Since 2019 I've worked on the human side of AI. I write the prompts and edge cases that test large language models, rate and rewrite their answers against client rubrics, and label video, audio and image data. Right now I do this for Turing and micro1.

01

LLM evaluation & RLHF

Rating and ranking model responses, rubric-based scoring, writing SFT data, and spotting edge cases for quality benchmarks.

02

Adversarial & safety testing

Designing prompts and scenarios that make models fail, then explaining each failure in structured written feedback, including in JSON.

03

Video & audio annotation

Frame-by-frame object tracking, action recognition, temporal segmentation, and time-stamped transcription for speech-recognition (ASR) training.

04

Quality control

Reviewing other annotators' batches, correcting mislabels, measuring inter-annotator agreement, and building error taxonomies.
